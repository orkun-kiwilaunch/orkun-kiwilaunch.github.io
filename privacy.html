egg privacy policy

egg is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner. It has no other users.

With the owner's consent, egg reads the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, and creates or updates events in the owner's own calendar.

The data is stored on the owner's own machine. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is used only to assist the owner.

egg's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: use the support email shown on egg's Google consent screen.
